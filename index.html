Our story

A neighbourhood shop that takes the craft seriously.

We opened on Market Street in 2012 with a simple idea: proper barbering shouldn't feel stiff. Come in for a skin fade or a full hot-towel shave and you'll get the same thing every time — an unhurried chair, a barber who listens, and a finish that still looks good a fortnight later.

Barber-led, but everyone's welcome. Men, women and little ones all sit in our chairs, and we're proud to be a small part of the town's week.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. We keep a few chairs for walk-ins each day, but weekends and Thursday/Friday evenings get busy — booking online guarantees your time and your barber.

Do you cut women's and children's hair?

Absolutely. We're barber-led but everyone's welcome — Priya looks after women's cuts, colour and blow-dries, and the whole team is great with little ones.

Where can I park?

There's pay-and-display parking on Market Street and the free Sutcliffe Street car park is a two-minute walk. Heywood bus interchange is just around the corner.

How should I pay?

Cash, card and contactless are all fine — including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Tips are always appreciated but never expected.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

We do — a hot-towel shave makes a cracking gift. Pop in or mention it when you book and we'll sort a voucher for any service or amount.
