About us Since 2012

A neighbourhood shop that takes the craft seriously.

Every cut comes two ways. The quiet chair, or the chatty one. Same price, your call, and no need to explain yourself.

FAQs Good to know

Good to know

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. We keep a few chairs for walk-ins each day, though weekends and Thursday and Friday evenings get busy, so booking online guarantees your time and your barber.

Do you cut women's and children's hair?

Absolutely. We're barber-led but everyone's welcome. Priya handles women's cuts, colour and blow-dries, and the whole team is brilliant with little ones.

Where can I park?

There's pay-and-display parking on Market Street and the free Sutcliffe Street car park is a two-minute walk. Heywood bus interchange is just around the corner.

How should I pay?

Cash, card and contactless all work here, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Tips are always appreciated but never expected.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

We do. A hot-towel shave makes a cracking gift, so pop in or mention it when you book and we'll sort a voucher for any service or amount.

What's the quiet chair?

Some days you want a chat, some days you just want to switch off. Tell us "quiet chair" when you book, or the chatty one if you fancy the banter. Same cut, same price, no awkwardness either way.
